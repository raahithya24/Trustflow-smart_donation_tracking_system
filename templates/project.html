Why Trust Us

Security Built Into Every Layer

End-to-End Encryption

All donation data encrypted in transit and at rest. Passwords use scrypt hashing — never stored plain.

3-Layer Fraud Detection

Duplicate checks, velocity limits, and unusual amount detection block bad actors before they succeed.

Full Audit Trail

Every change to every record is logged with timestamp and user — nothing is ever silently edited.

Admin-Verified NGOs

Every NGO goes through admin review before appearing to donors. Trust badges are earned, not automatic.

Expense Transparency

NGOs log every expense. Donors see exactly how their money was used — down to the last rupee.

Role-Based Access

Donors, NGOs, and admins each see only what they should. No data leaks across roles ever.
